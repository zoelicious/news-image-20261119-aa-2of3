# Thursday 19 November 2026 — The World in One Day (Aa, 2/3)

[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261119/zoelicious/news-image-20261119-aa-2of3/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/news-image-20261119/zoelicious/news-image-20261119-aa-2of3/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)

One image that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in world news: 35 stories
across every continent — a first-ever trilateral in Shenzhen, a genocide
verdict in The Hague, Anthropic's record IPO on its first day of trading,
and the rest of the day.

Layout ("Anthropic Hero"): classic cream front page with a giant serif date,
a day-in-numbers strip and a region count; three drawn scene heroes
(APEC, World Court, Anthropic); a full-width COP31 featured banner; ten
drawn scene cards; a small flat world map with numbered pins; 21 one-line
briefs; and a DATE CHECK box listing the other days' news that was
deliberately left out (18 Nov: Van Gogh, Jeffries, Voyager 1, EU €45bn loan,
Nvidia, Pew 2035, Anthropic IPO pricing; 20 Nov: COP31 close, Cyclone
Akvan's landfall). Every story was reported on 19 November 2026; claims
are attributed, and each card carries a REPORTED / CLAIM tag with its source.
Original drawings, drawn in code.

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[Sum up 19 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119).

`main.py` (standard library only) copies the committed `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. `render.py` (Pillow, build time only)
draws `entry.png` from the story list in that file.
